Permission flow

Two quick permissions

So we can tell you when to get off.

Precise location

To know which station you're at. Stations are less than 1 km apart, so approximate location isn't enough.

Notifications

To show your next stop and buzz before you need to get off.

Only during a trip

Location is used only while a trip is running and never leaves your phone.
